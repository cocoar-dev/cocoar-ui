Accessibility

Cards are semantic containers that group related content. Key considerations:

  • Cards are rendered as block-level elements without implicit ARIA roles
  • Use appropriate heading hierarchy within cards (h3, h4)
  • Interactive cards should use role="button" or be wrapped in <a>
  • Ensure sufficient color contrast for all card variants
  • Content within cards remains keyboard accessible
