Variants

Choose the appropriate variant based on the action's importance and context.

Primary Secondary Tertiary Danger Ghost
Primary

Main call-to-action. Use sparingly, typically once per view.

 Secondary

Alternative actions. Pairs well with primary buttons.

 Tertiary

Low-emphasis actions with brand color hint.

 Danger

Destructive actions like delete or remove.

 Ghost

Minimal emphasis, often for cancel or dismiss.

Loading State

Show a loading spinner while an async action is in progress.

Loading Behavior

  • With icon: The spinner replaces the icon position, keeping the button text visible.
  • Without icon: The spinner overlays the center while the text fades out.

This ensures the button maintains its size and prevents layout shifts.
