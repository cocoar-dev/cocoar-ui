Note

Notes are callout components for highlighting important information, tips, warnings, or contextual messages. They feature a colored left border and subtle background to draw attention without being intrusive.

Card vs Note

When to use each component.

Card

  • General content containers
  • Grouping related information
  • Full border around content
  • Optional elevation (shadow)
  • Layout and structure

Note

  • Callouts and highlighted info
  • Tips, warnings, errors
  • Left border accent only
  • Subtle colored background
  • Drawing attention to content
