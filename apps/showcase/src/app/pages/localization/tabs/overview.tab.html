Three Independent Choices

Every formatted value depends on three independent choices. Most systems conflate them — this system resolves all three explicitly.

Format the shape

Controls structure: order, separators, numeric vs textual representation.

dd.MM.yyyy → 14.02.2026
MM/dd/yyyy → 02/14/2026

Language the words

Controls which words appear: month names, weekday names.

en → January, Monday
de → Januar, Montag

Region the conventions

Controls cultural expectations: first day of week, date order.

de-DE → Januar
de-AT → Jänner

BCP 47 Language Tags

Both simple language codes and full BCP 47 tags are supported.

German variants

Tag Resolves to Output
de de-DE (implicit) Januar, 14.01.2026
de-AT de-AT (explicit) Jänner, 14.01.2026
de-DE de-DE (explicit) Januar, 14.01.2026

English variants

Tag Resolves to Output
en en-US (implicit) January, 01/14/2026
en-GB en-GB (explicit) January, 14/01/2026
setLanguage('de') and setLanguage('de-DE') both work, but they are not identical.
Bare 'de' lets the browser assume a region.
Use the full BCP 47 tag when the region matters — e.g., Austrian German (de-AT) uses "Jänner" instead of "Januar".
